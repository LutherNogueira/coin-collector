# Asset credits

## Pixel Adventure 1 (Pixel Frog)

All images here come, unmodified, from **Pixel Adventure 1** by Pixel Frog:

- `characters/ninja_frog/*.png`: the Ninja Frog animations (idle, run, jump, fall, hit; 32×32 frames).
- `items/fruits/*.png`: the fruit animations (17 frames each, 32×32) and the "collected" burst (6 frames).
- `terrain/terrain.png`: the 16×16 terrain tileset (the grass-topped block is used).
- `terrain/spikes.png`: the 16×16 spike tile.
- `background/blue.png`: the 64×64 "Blue" background tile.

- Source: <https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/pixel-adventure-1> (`Pixel Adventure 1.zip`, 204 kB)
- License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0), as stated on the author's page. Attribution is not required.
- The pack is also listed on OpenGameArt (<https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-adventure-1>) with a CC-BY 4.0 label.
  The two pages disagree, so this credit is given anyway.
- The zip itself contains no license file; the license comes from the itch.io page.

## Sound effects

`sounds/*.wav` (jump, collect, death, win) are synthesized by `tools/generate_sfx.py` from plain square, triangle,
saw and noise waves. They are original to this project and contain no third-party audio.

## Music

`music/jungle_prince.mp3` is **"Jungle Prince"** by iamoneabe, unmodified.

- Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/jungle-prince>
- License: CC0 (public domain), as listed on the page, which also says no attribution is necessary.
